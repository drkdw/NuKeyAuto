How it works

Get started in under a minute.

Get your API token

Create a token at web.nuki.io and copy your Smart Lock ID.

Enter in Settings

Open NuKey Auto, go to Settings, and paste your credentials.

Connect to your car

Plug in your phone and NuKey Auto appears in Android Auto.

Tap to control

Open or lock your door from your car screen.
